A clear start with solar

Panels collect sunlight. An inverter converts their output into electricity your home can use. A site assessment helps establish a suitable layout.

  • Roof condition, usable area and shade
  • Current electricity use and supply
  • Equipment location and grid requirements

Start with the roof.
Keep the whole home in mind.

Good decisions begin with useful questions. Here is a practical route from curiosity to an installer conversation.

  1. 1

    Get to know your roof

    Gather what you know about its age, material, available space and nearby shade. There is no need to climb up.

  2. 2

    Talk about your everyday

    Think about when your home uses electricity and whether future changes, like an electric car, belong in the plan.

  3. 3

    Ask for a site-led proposal

    A qualified installer can assess suitability, explain equipment and confirm approvals, costs and installation details.

Good questions

            
Let’s clear
 a few things up.

          

          

            

              
                Is every roof suitable for solar?
              
              

                

                  Suitability depends on roof condition, structure, orientation,
                  shade, available space and local requirements. A qualified
                  installer needs to assess your property before recommending a
                  system.
                

              

            

            

              
                Do I need to include a battery?
              
              

                

                  A battery is a separate option to discuss, not an automatic
                  part of every project. Ask your installer how storage would
                  fit your household, equipment, intended use and budget. Backup
                  operation needs specific compatible design.
                

              

            

            

              
                What should I have ready for a conversation?
              
              

                

                  A general location, what you know about the roof and your
                  goals are useful starting points. Your installer may later ask
                  for electricity usage and property details through their own
                  secure process. Do not upload sensitive documents to this
                  demo.
                

              

            

            

              
                Can this page tell me what I will save?
              
              

                

                  No. This page does not estimate generation, savings, payback
                  or incentives. Those depend on a specific design, your usage,
                  local conditions and current rules. Ask for a clear written
                  proposal and check its assumptions.
                

              

            

            

              
                Does preparing a brief book a visit?
              
              

                

                  No. The brief is created locally and remains unsent. Nothing
                  is booked, submitted or saved by Solhaven. You choose whether
                  and how to share it with an installer.
